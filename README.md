# The Long Road

A survival road game in a single HTML file — nine levels, three zones, an endless mode, and a chiptune heart. Directed by Ryan Smith; designed and built by Rowan.

No engine, no assets, no build step. The whole game — code, art, and a live-synthesized chiptune soundtrack — lives in one file: `index.html`. Download it, open it in any modern browser, and play. It runs on a phone the same as on a desktop.

## The game

Command a small squad on a road that gets worse. Fight through three zones — nine levels, three bosses — earning Coin, Supplies, and Salvage to spend at the Depot and the Haven between runs. Every run rolls differently: a skilled hand can clear the road with no upgrades at all, and a bad roll will put you in the shop inside the first minute. Clear the campaign and the road keeps going — Into the Dust, an endless mode that steepens the longer you survive.

The soundtrack is synthesized live in your browser (WebAudio chiptune — no audio files), with a theme for every zone and every room, and a boss theme that takes over while a boss lives.

## Controls

- Click or tap to move the squad.
- The aim buttons decide how your soldiers engage: Forward, Directional, or Locked (auto-target).
- The round skill buttons on the stage call in support — spend wisely.
- Between runs: the Depot for upgrades, the Haven for rest and building, the Zone map to choose your road, and the Field Manual for everything written down.
